# Core Message Real-Material Usability Protocol

## Participant task

Bring a product you understand well but struggle to explain in one sentence. Add only material you are authorized to use. Without coaching on which answer is “right,” inspect the two directions, open their evidence, edit if needed, Apply one, and use it in the generated deck opening.

## Observe, do not infer

- Can the participant explain the difference between mechanism and human tension?
- Which direction do they inspect first and why?
- What exact words do they edit before Apply?
- Can they tell preview from Apply and recognize what downstream content will change?
- When shown history/stale status, can they recover without assistance?
- Do they reuse the applied message in an actual brief, homepage, pitch, or client discussion?

## Record

Participant code; product category; material type; chosen lens; original output; final edited output; evidence opened; assistance; time to Apply; downstream artifact; later reuse; verbatim reaction; observed failure.

## Product gate

Run with 3–5 target participants. Do not report success from intent alone. A provisional pass requires at least 3 participants, at least 2 independently reaching Apply, no participant confusing preview with Apply, and at least 1 using the result in a real downstream artifact. Report every participant and every abandonment.
